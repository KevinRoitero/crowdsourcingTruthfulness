# Rights and Reuse

This release contains research material with different rights status.

## Study Data

The original S6 data and README retain their original bytes. The detailed tables contain participant judgments, comments, questionnaire responses and search activity. They are provided for research and reproducibility. Participant contributions do not automatically fall under the license for material created by the authors.

## Task Materials

The task materials include the original assignment design and questionnaire, and supporting interface documentation from the longitudinal study. Statement text and source details may belong to third parties. The file workers.json contains no platform worker identifiers. This does not imply that the original task had no participation restrictions.

## PolitiFact and Search Results

PolitiFact statements, ratings and metadata, search results, URLs, titles and snippets retain their original rights. This release does not grant rights over content owned by third parties.

## Privacy

The release uses pseudonyms for worker identifiers. Do not attempt to identify or contact participants, or combine records with other data to identify them.
