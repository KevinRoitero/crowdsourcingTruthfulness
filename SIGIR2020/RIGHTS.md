# Rights and Reuse

This release contains research material with different rights status.

The availability of data for research does not mean that every part of the package is covered by the same license or that unrestricted reuse is permitted. See `LICENSE.md`.

## Study Data and Historical Sources

The package preserves original publication material, detailed crowdsourcing records and task-related data. Historical files are retained in `Historical Data/` without changing their original rights status.

The reconstructed release provides supporting documentation, mappings and integrity metadata. Their inclusion does not grant new rights over earlier source material.

## Fact Check Source Material

`PolitiFact-Ground_Truth.csv` and `RMIT_ABC_Fact_Check-Ground_Truth.csv` contain statements, labels and metadata from their respective fact-check sources.

Original fact-check content and other third-party material retain the rights and reuse conditions of the corresponding sources.

## Crowdsourcing Task and Research Instruments

`Crowdsourcing Task/` contains historical task assignments and supporting study materials. Questions, instruments and external statement content may have separate authors or rights holders.

The historical task files are preserved as part of the study record; their inclusion does not automatically grant further reuse rights.

## Search Results and Website Content

`DataFrame/workers_urls.csv` records participant searches, result titles, snippets, URLs and related information.

Some of this content originates from search providers and external websites. Including it for research and reproducibility does not transfer rights in the original content. Users should check applicable licenses and terms for their intended use.

## Human Research Data and Privacy

The release contains pseudonymous participant judgments, comments, questionnaire responses, task completions and search activity.

Internal worker identifiers preserve study links but do not guarantee anonymity. Do not attempt to identify or contact participants or combine records with external information to identify individuals.

No reversible worker-identity mapping or private result archive is distributed. Screening of the released records cannot guarantee that every combination of fields is non-identifying.

## Provenance and Integrity

`release_manifest.json` identifies source materials and package file properties. `checksums.csv` provides file sizes and SHA256 values.

The earlier published material is preserved as historical provenance. This documentation clarification does not modify the scientific data or assign a new license.

## License Scope

No blanket license is asserted for the release. Participant contributions, fact-check content, search-provider material, website content, research instruments and historical third-party material retain their applicable rights.
