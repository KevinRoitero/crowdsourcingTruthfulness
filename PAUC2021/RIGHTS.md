# Rights and Reuse

This release contains research material with different rights status.

The presence of a file in the package does not mean that every part of that file is covered by the same license.

## Material Created for This Release

Original documentation, release metadata, and other material created by the release authors are available under CC BY 4.0 where the authors have the right to grant that license.

See `LICENSE.md`.

## Study Data

The release contains judgments, comments, questionnaire responses, dimension selections, and search activity collected during the study.

These records are included to support research and reproducibility. Participant contributions do not automatically become available under the license for material created by the release authors.

The original study data are preserved without modification.

## PolitiFact Material

`PolitiFact-Ground_Truth.csv` contains statements, truthfulness labels, URLs, and other information from PolitiFact.

This material keeps its original rights status. The release does not grant new rights over PolitiFact content.

## Search Results and Website Content

`DataFrame/workers_urls.csv` contains searches and results recorded during the study, including URLs, titles, and snippets.

Some information comes from external search services and websites. These sources retain their own rights and terms of use.

Including this material for research does not transfer rights in the original content. Users should check whether their intended reuse is permitted.

## Privacy and Responsible Reuse

The release contains pseudonymous participant records.

Stable worker identifiers help connect records across collection rounds, but they do not guarantee that participants cannot be identified.

Do not attempt to identify or contact participants. Do not combine these records with external information to identify individuals.

## License Scope

`LICENSE.md` applies only to material for which the release authors have the right to grant the stated license.

Participant contributions, PolitiFact material, search results, website content, and other material owned by third parties keep their original rights status.
